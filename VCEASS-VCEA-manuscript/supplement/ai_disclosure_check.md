## IEEE reminder

IEEE requires disclosure in the acknowledgments when AI-generated content, including text, figures, images, or code, is included in the article. The disclosure should identify the AI system, the specific sections affected, and the level of use.

## Recommendation

Confirm with all authors/advisor before submission whether an IEEE AI disclosure statement is required and, if so, what scope it should cover.
